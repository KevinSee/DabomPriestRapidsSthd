Evaluation of the Utility of Some PIT Tag Sites

Wenatchee

Author
Affiliation

Kevin E. See

Published

Goal

Our goal is to evaluate the impact of removing several PIT tag detection sites in the Wenatchee basin on steelhead escapement estimates made with the Dam Adult Branch Occupancy Model (DABOM). Specifically, we examined the effects of removing CHL (in the upper Chiwawa River), NAL (in upper Nason Creek) and PEU (in upper Peshastin Creek).

Methods

We used the exact same PIT tag detections that were used in the latest DABOM estimates. We chose several years to test across a range of escapement sizes (2011, 2014, 2017, 2022. We removed any detections from CHL, NAL or PEU, and dropped those three sites from the model. We then re-ran DABOM and compiled estimates of detection probability and abundance at the site immediately downstream of those sites to compare with the original estimates (Table 1). We plotted the differences, and calculated summary statistics.

Discussion

Overall, removing these three upper sites had little effect on detection probability and escapement estimates. In general, the updated detection probabilities were sometimes biased slightly high, leading to escapement estimates that were biased slightly low. In the Chiwawa River in 2022, there were no detections on the CHL_U node, which forced the model to fix detection probability of the CHL_D node to 100%, leading to an underestimate of escapement to the Chiwawa. When the upper site, CHU, was included, detection probability of CHL_D could be estimated at 33% (Table 3).

An upper site provides the most information to the model when it contains detections of tags that were not detected at the lower site. This happened only a handful of times in our example years (Table 2). When the upper site did not detect any tags that weren’t also detected at the lower site, the estimates of detection probability and escapement at the lower site weren’t affected at all (other than slight variation due to MCMC sampling runs).

If the upper sites are removed, it is important for these results that the lower site be maintained such that the two arrays are as independent as possible, to meet the assumptions of DABOM.
